Controls & Driving Guide

Webcam Hand Steering (Primary)

1. Hold both hands up in view of your camera as if gripping an imaginary wheel at 9 and 3 o'clock.

2. Rotate both hands counter-clockwise to steer left, or clockwise to steer right.

3. Spread both palms wide open to brake (configurable in Calibration settings).

If a hand leaves the camera frame, steering safely eases back toward center until both hands return.
